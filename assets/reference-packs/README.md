# RPG reference assets

These packs are kept here for local prototyping. The two Gridwright free samples are loaded by the game and may be shipped inside a finished game or app. Do not redistribute them as standalone asset packs. Credit is appreciated, not required: “Art by Gridwright.” See each included `LICENSE.txt` for the full terms.

| Folder / archive | Contents | Source and license |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Serene_Village_revamped_v1.9/` | LimeZu village tiles, houses, terrain, animated props | [LimeZu Serene Village](https://limezu.itch.io/serenevillagerevamped), CC BY 4.0. Credit LimeZu if used. |
| `medieval-village-tileset-16x16-pixel-art-fantasy-free-sample/` | Gridwright medieval village sample | [Gridwright Medieval Village](https://v3su.itch.io/medieval-village-tileset-16x16-pixel-art-fantasy), free sample only; the complete pack is not included. Personal and commercial game use is permitted; do not redistribute the files. |
| `top-down-rpg-characters-16x16-pixel-art-free-sample/` | Two Gridwright characters and emotes | [Gridwright Top-Down Characters](https://v3su.itch.io/top-down-rpg-characters-16x16-pixel-art), free sample only; the complete pack is not included. Personal and commercial game use is permitted; do not redistribute the files. |
| `kenney_roguelike-rpg-pack/` | Kenney RPG tiles, props, and sample maps | [Kenney Roguelike/RPG pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/roguelike-rpg-pack), CC0. |
| `kenney_roguelike-characters/` | Kenney character sprites and sheets | [Kenney Roguelike Characters](https://kenney.nl/assets/roguelike-characters), CC0. |

The matching `.zip` archives are retained alongside the extracted folders. Only the Gridwright free samples are currently loaded by the game. LimeZu's CC BY 4.0 village pack and the Kenney CC0 packs are local references and are not currently used in gameplay.
