GRIDWRIGHT MEDIEVAL VILLAGE - FREE SAMPLE  v1.0

93 pieces from Gridwright Medieval Village: grass (4), the 47-tile cobblestone road, the thatch roof set
(9 pieces + 2 dormers), the timber-frame facade set (24 pieces), 3 shop signs, a barrel, a crate, a lamp post and an

THE BITMASK
  cobble_NNN.png: N=1, NE=2, E=4, SE=8, S=16, SW=32, W=64, NW=128. A diagonal counts only when the two neighbours
  next to it are set. village.json lists every piece with its sheet position.

A HOUSE
  Roof: ridge row on top, any number of slope rows, eave row at the bottom; _left, _middle, _right per row.
  Under it, one facade row per storey (doors and shop counters on the ground row).
